Metro Project

Nagpur Metro System Integration

This project involved comprehensive dynamic reactive power compensation commissioning and system integration for the Nagpur Metro rail infrastructure. The scope included complex master/slave network coordination and resolving extensive communication challenges on site.

Deep Dive Details

  • 150 kVAr × 27, 225 kVAr × 2 and 450 kVAr × 5 systems fully commissioned.
  • Executed flawless Master/slave and network coordination across multiple RSS/TSS locations.
  • Maintained critical Power Factor (PF) performance around 0.98–0.998 during all phases of commissioning.
  • Successfully worked through and resolved PT feedback irregularities.
  • Troubleshot and repaired RTU/SCADA communication interface issues to ensure seamless data flow to central control.
